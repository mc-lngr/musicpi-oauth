Privacy Policy for MusicPi Backup

Last updated: October 14, 2026

MusicPi Backup is a private, non-commercial application operated solely for its owner's personal use. It is not offered to the public as a service.

Google user data accessed

MusicPi Backup uses Google Drive authorization to upload backup files created by the application to the owner's Google Drive account. The application is configured to use the Google Drive drive.file scope.

How the data is used

Google Drive access is used only to create and manage files that MusicPi Backup uploads for the purpose of off-device backup. Google user data is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials required for the automated backup are stored privately on the owner's own system and are not published in this website or its source repository. MusicPi Backup does not sell Google user data and does not share Google user data with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Backup files stored in Google Drive remain under the owner's control and can be deleted by the owner from Google Drive. Google account access can also be revoked by the owner through the Google account's security settings.

Contact

Questions about this private application can be directed to the support email address displayed on its Google OAuth consent screen.

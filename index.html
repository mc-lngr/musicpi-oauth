MusicPi Backup

MusicPi Backup is a private, non-commercial application used by its owner to create off-device backups of a personal MusicPi SQLite music database.

Google Drive use

The application uses Google Drive only to upload backup files created by the owner to the owner's own Google Drive account. It is not offered as a service to other users.
